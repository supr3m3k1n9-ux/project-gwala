# Project Gwala Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-08-07

This file is the authoritative phased roadmap for Project Gwala. The roadmap
remains frozen unless evidence earns a change and the Investment Committee
records that decision in `DECISION_LOG.md`.

## North Star

Build a quantitative trading firm that reaches sustainable profitability as
quickly as reasonably possible.

Current operating mode:

```text
EXECUTE
```

## Phase 1 - Build the Firm

Objective:

Create the research, paper-validation, reporting, and operational foundation
required to collect decision-quality evidence.

Status:

```text
substantially complete
```

Activation condition:

Already active historically. Continue maintaining only what protects evidence
collection, safety, and reporting integrity.

What belongs here:

- local research framework,
- Webull market-data workflow,
- scanner,
- Paper Gate,
- Contract Gate,
- validation/accounting,
- reporting,
- production health checks,
- company memory.

## Phase 2 - Discover the First Commercially Viable Trading Edge

Objective:

Use finite research runway to discover the firm's first strategy worthy of
becoming a revenue-producing trading system.

Status:

```text
current phase
```

Activation condition:

Current company state.

Primary work:

- preserve VWAP official paper validation,
- collect Morning SPY/QQQ Long ORB Manual Paper-Watch evidence,
- evaluate decision triggers,
- avoid speculative engineering,
- maintain clean evidence ledgers.

Phase 2 exit condition:

A strategy earns commercial readiness and approval for Tiny Live.

## Phase 3 - Commercial Validation: Tiny Live

Objective:

Validate a commercially ready strategy with tiny live capital under strict
capital-preservation controls.

Status:

```text
future, not active
```

Activation condition:

One strategy earns Investment Committee approval for Tiny Live based on
paper/manual evidence, risk profile, opportunity frequency, operational
reliability, and commercial viability.

What belongs here:

- tiny live account,
- strict position sizing,
- daily/weekly loss limits,
- kill switch,
- live execution audit,
- scale-up/scale-down criteria.

Explicitly not active now:

- real-money trading,
- broker order placement,
- live autonomous execution.

## Phase 3.5 - Project Gwala Command Center

Objective:

Build the CEO and Investment Committee command center that manages live
operations, research, capital allocation, reporting, and strategy oversight
before scaling.

Status:

```text
future, not active
```

Activation condition:

The first strategy is approved for Tiny Live and before capital scaling begins.

Reason:

The Command Center should manage the transition from research to live capital.
It is not intended to help discover the first edge.

Explicitly not active now:

- dashboard redesign,
- executive OS build,
- command-center architecture expansion.

## Phase 4 - Scale the First Revenue Engine

Objective:

Scale the first commercially validated strategy while preserving capital and
operational reliability.

Status:

```text
future, not active
```

Activation condition:

Tiny Live evidence confirms the strategy remains commercially viable after real
fills, slippage, costs, execution timing, and operational constraints.

What belongs here:

- Small Live,
- Medium Live,
- Full Production,
- progressive capital scaling,
- drawdown-based scale-down rules.

## Phase 5 - Portfolio Expansion Inside Equities

Objective:

Add additional equities/options strategies only after the first revenue engine
has proven the firm can validate and operate one strategy commercially.

Status:

```text
future-only
```

Activation condition:

The first revenue engine is operating profitably enough that additional
research allocation has higher expected return than scaling/maintaining the
first engine.

## Phase 6 - Operational Hardening for a Growing Firm

Objective:

Harden infrastructure, monitoring, reporting, accounting, deployment, and
operational controls around a firm that has live capital and revenue.

Status:

```text
future-only
```

Activation condition:

Live operations and scaling create evidence-backed infrastructure needs.

## Phase 7 - Multi-Asset Quantitative Trading

Objective:

Expand Project Gwala beyond US equities/options into additional asset classes.

First approved future expansion target:

```text
Crypto perpetual futures on Hyperliquid
```

Status:

```text
future-only; no work authorized
```

Activation condition:

The Equities Division produces the firm's first commercially validated revenue
engine.

Guardrail:

No crypto engineering, research, capital allocation, playbook development, or
implementation work is authorized before the activation condition is met.
