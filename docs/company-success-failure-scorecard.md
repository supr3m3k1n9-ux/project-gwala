# Project Gwala Company Success & Failure Scorecard

Purpose: keep Project Gwala from drifting. Each category defines what success
looks like, what failure looks like, and what action should happen when a
condition is crossed.

Mission:

```text
Build a quantitative trading firm that reaches sustainable profitability as
quickly as reasonably possible.
```

Default operating mode:

```text
EXECUTE
```

Current phase:

```text
Phase 2 - Discover the firm's first commercially viable trading edge.
```

Weekly Executive Reports should include this scorecard as a standing section.
When a failure condition is crossed, the report should name the category,
trigger, and action. When no trigger is crossed, the report should state:

```text
No scorecard trigger crossed. Continue execution.
```

## Scorecard

| Category | Current Status | Success Criteria | Failure Criteria | Trigger Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company Survival | Survival / Pre-Revenue. Operating cost is expected to be covered by founder capital until trading profit exists. | The company has at least 6 months of operating runway or monthly trading profit covers recurring operating cost. | Runway falls below 3 months, monthly operating cost rises without a clear evidence-collection benefit, or the company cannot continue the approved research workflow. | Cut nonessential costs, freeze unearned engineering, and focus only on the highest-probability path to a commercially validated edge. |
| Research Success | Evidence collection active. Primary checkpoint remains completed official paper trades. | The firm reaches the current decision checkpoint with enough clean official paper trades and no unresolved data/accounting integrity issue. | Evidence velocity projects more than 8 calendar weeks to the next decision checkpoint, or collected evidence is too inconsistent to support promotion/reduction/retirement decisions. | Prioritize evidence velocity review. Keep strategy rules unchanged unless evidence proves the bottleneck is operational rather than market-driven. |
| Strategy Success | VWAP remains primary active paper-watch lane. ORB is funded secondary shadow-only lane. | At least one strategy shows positive decision-quality expectancy, acceptable drawdown, sufficient opportunity frequency, and operational reliability across the approved evidence gates. | A funded strategy fails its retirement trigger, remains negative after required matured evidence, or cannot produce enough qualified opportunities to be commercially viable. | Reduce or retire the strategy according to approved triggers and reallocate research time only to the highest expected return on research time. |
| Engineering Success | Engineering is constrained to evidence-earned fixes and operational wiring. | Production stays GREEN/WATCH, evidence collection is not blocked, and no engineering change alters strategy/risk/trading behavior without approval. | A production or reporting defect causes lost candidates, missed Contract Gate opportunities, accounting inconsistency, or invalid evidence. | Recommend INVESTIGATE or BUILD only when the evidence shows engineering has higher expected return than continued evidence collection. |
| Commercial Success | Pre-Revenue. No live trading capital is deployed. | Monthly realized trading profit covers the estimated monthly operating cost, then progresses toward self-funded growth while respecting drawdown limits. | A strategy reaches live testing but real fills, slippage, costs, or operational errors erase the paper edge; or monthly losses breach approved live-stage limits. | Halt or scale down capital deployment, review execution quality, and return to paper/shadow evidence until commercial assumptions are restored. |
| Firm Success | Single-division equities research firm in Phase 2. Multi-asset expansion is future-only. | The Equities Division produces the firm's first commercially validated revenue engine, with repeatable evidence, controlled drawdown, and clean operations. | No strategy becomes commercially validated after exhausting approved research triggers, or the firm expands architecture/research before the Equities Division earns revenue. | Stay in EXECUTE mode, prevent speculative expansion, and make exactly one Investment Committee recommendation: CONTINUE, INVESTIGATE, BUILD, or REALLOCATE. |

## Operating Notes

- Success means progress toward sustainable profitability, not more code.
- Failure means a predefined business condition was crossed, not a bad day.
- Engineering is successful when it protects or accelerates decision-quality
  evidence collection without adding unnecessary surface area.
- Research capital should move only when a trigger says another path has higher
  expected return on research time.
- Crypto perpetual futures on Hyperliquid remain a future Phase 7 roadmap item
  only. No research, engineering, or implementation work is authorized until the
  Equities Division produces the firm's first commercially validated revenue
  engine.
